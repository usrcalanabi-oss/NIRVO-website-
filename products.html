GOOD TO KNOW / NIRVO FAQ

Clear answers.
No surprises.

We want every enquiry to start with realistic expectations.

Are these products ready to purchase?

The catalogue currently presents proposed product concepts. It does not mean every item is manufactured, stocked or ready to ship. We confirm development and availability individually.

Are the displayed prices final?

No. Prices are indicative starting references only. The final quote depends on the selected components, configuration, development work, delivery location and applicable charges.

How do I request a product quote?

Add a concept to your enquiry cart or contact NIRVO with the product name, intended use and requirements. We can then review the scope and discuss a suitable configuration.

Can I request a custom design?

Yes, you can submit a custom project or engineering requirement. We will review the details first; feasibility, cost and timeline need to be agreed before any work is accepted.

When is an enquiry considered an order?

Adding an item to the cart or sending an enquiry does not confirm an order. An order should proceed only after you receive and approve a written scope, final quote, timeline and payment terms.

Can I pay online on this website?

Online checkout and payment are not currently enabled. Please wait for a confirmed written quotation and official payment instructions from NIRVO before making any payment.
